The Wayfarer's
Code of Conduct

We are building more than a map; we are cultivating a community of modern travelers. These guidelines ensure our shared journey remains safe, respectful, and premium.

handshake

Respect

Core Value 01

Treat every member of the TripAutostrade community with the dignity they deserve. We do not tolerate harassment, hate speech, or exclusionary behavior. Whether you are reviewing a service station or interacting in a thread, keep the dialogue constructive and sophisticated.

verified_user

Honesty

Core Value 02

Your reviews guide others. Be truthful about wait times, cleanliness, and service quality. Avoid embellishment or malicious reporting. Authenticity is the currency of the Modern Wayfarer.

block

No Spam

Core Value 03

The feed is for high-value information. Do not use the platform for commercial solicitation, repetitive content, or irrelevant media. Keep the "Digital Concierge" experience clean and utility-focused.

visibility_off

Safety & Privacy

Core Value 04

Never share personal identification of others without consent. Use caution when sharing your real-time location. Safety on the highway begins with digital safety in the app.

Detailed Terms of Engagement

By participating in the TripAutostrade community, you acknowledge that your contributions become part of a shared collective designed to enhance the travel experience for everyone. We reserve the right to curate and moderate content that does not align with the "Digital Concierge" aesthetic or functional requirements.

Moderation is performed by a combination of automated linguistic analysis and human review by our community curators. If your content is flagged, it will be reviewed within 24 hours. Repeated violations of the Respect or Honesty clauses may result in permanent revocation of Wayfarer status and account suspension.

Intellectual property rights for your uploaded photos remain yours, however, by uploading them to the platform, you grant TripAutostrade a non-exclusive, perpetual license to display these images within the app context and marketing materials meant to highlight the community's vibrancy.

We encourage travelers to document their journey, but remind you to never capture photos or post reviews while operating a vehicle. Safety is paramount. Any content clearly captured while driving will be removed for the protection of all highway users.
